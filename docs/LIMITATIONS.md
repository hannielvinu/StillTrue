# Limits and privacy

- Only enabled domain packs and accessible official online evidence are covered. Initial domain activation must be completed after network access is available.
- Official pages may lag real policy, apply to another jurisdiction/year, or contain ambiguous text.
- Models may agree on an error. Quote presence proves provenance, not correct interpretation.
- PII patterns are incomplete; remove personal and confidential information yourself.
- Pasted text is temporary job data. Evidence caches may contain sensitive text from public pages. Review upstream provider retention and gateway logging policies.
- Tavily accounting reserves conservative estimated credits, including failed retries; it may stop before the provider's actual daily bill reaches the cap. It is not a financial guarantee.
- No authenticated public deployment, distributed jobs, calibrated accuracy claim or production SLA is provided.
- Unofficial source disagreement warnings are not generated: we avoid attributing contradictions without separately validating secondary evidence.
- Corrected answers are optional and conservative; full semantic preservation cannot be guaranteed. Original text remains visible.
- Not legal, tax, financial or medical advice. Verify consequential decisions directly.
